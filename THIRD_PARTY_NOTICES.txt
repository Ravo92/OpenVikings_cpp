FFmpeg
======
OpenVikings uses FFmpeg's libavformat, libavcodec, libavutil, libswscale and
libswresample to decode the original Cultures 2 Bink movies and their audio.
The FFmpeg libraries are statically linked; no proprietary Bink SDK or DLL is
included. Original game movies remain user-provided game data.

GothicKit DirectMusic
====================
The native x64 music backend statically links GothicKit/dmusic and its bundled
TinySoundFont implementation to synthesize the original SGT scores and DLS
instruments. Original game music and the Windows General MIDI collection remain
user-provided data; they are not distributed in this repository.

Upstream: https://github.com/GothicKit/dmusic
Pinned upstream commit: b73b270b94714b8ec8d9d0b2a9f3a5c445da52d1
